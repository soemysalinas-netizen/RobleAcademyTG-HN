Our History

Our institution has a long history dedicated to education, personal development, and service to the community.

Throughout the years, our organization has continued to grow, providing students with new opportunities and improving its educational programs.

Important Events

Beginning

Our institution started with few classes, no computer class, and not as many people.

Growth

As time went by, more classes began to be created, the computer class arrived, the volleyball academy began and even championships were held.

Expansion

Later on, there was more staff and more people; there was a taekwondo academy, and they used to go to championships.

Present Day

Due to some issues, the volleyball academy is smaller, and there are fewer people in the taekwondo academy too, though there are more classes now; the cafeteria has been enlarged, and there is a ballet studio and many other things.
